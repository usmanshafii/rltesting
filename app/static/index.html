    What this demonstrates, and what it does not

    • A detector trained only on synthetic, physics-based RGB + thermal images finds a KAL-class aircraft (MD-550 / L550E engine model) on commodity-camera frames, with frozen operating points.
    • Switching a camera off shows the single-band behaviour (models trained with random band dropout).
    • The empty-sky mode measures false alarms after track confirmation, the unit that matters for video.
    • Not shown: real cameras, birds, aircraft, clouds or terrain. Thermal and spectral properties are modelling assumptions. Numbers are from held-out synthetic scenes only.
